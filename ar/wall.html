See it on your wall

Point a phone at an empty wall and the piece appears there at its real size. Nothing to install. Sizes can be swapped before choosing one.

Lilac Vortex · 76 cm

The three guides are plain rings. They answer one question only: which size suits the wall.

On a computer the piece can be turned and inspected. The wall view needs a phone, so open this page there.

  • Pick a wall with something on it. A door, a window or a piece of furniture in view is enough. A camera reads an empty wall poorly.
  • Stand back about two metres.
  • Move the phone slowly across the wall for a few seconds, so the camera can read the surface.
  • Tap the wall to hang the piece, then walk closer.
  • Sizes are true. Nothing is scaled to flatter the work.
